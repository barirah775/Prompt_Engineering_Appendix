## Visible Architectural Details

- Roof form: pitch, gables, finials, chimneys  
- Wall materials: brick or stone type, colour, bonding, trim  
- Windows: shape, arches, sashes, mullions, tracery  
- Doors: arch shape, framing, panels  
- Ornamentation: buttresses, pilasters, string courses, carvings  
- Façade rhythm: bay count, spacing, alignment  
- Distinctive features: towers, dormers, turrets, belfries, signage

- Accurate texture for all materials  
- No perspective exaggeration  
- Undistorted proportions
