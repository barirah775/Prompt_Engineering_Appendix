# Template v1 — Interior  
## Baseline Floorplan Generation Template

---

### Proposed Floorplan Template

**Key Opening**  
Orthographic architectural floorplan

**Storey Specification**  
(e.g. single storey)

**Elevation / Perspective Specification**  
(e.g. front elevation, top-down)

**Scale**  
1:100

**Time Period**  
Medieval–Georgian (pre-1830s)

**Representation Scope**  
Short school + classroom description relevant to the historical period

**Structure**  
One sentence limited to:
- internal wall dimensions  
- external wall dimensions  
- placement logic  
- type of brick or stone construction  

**Further Detail**  
More descriptive sentence defining spatial logic, for example:
- classrooms along corridor  
- flanking central hall  
- vestibule and stair positioning  

**Additional Architectural Elements**  
Specify any required elements:
- staircases  
- porches  
- vestibules  
- apses  
- galleries  

---

### Conditions / Controls

- Doors and windows shown accurately  
- Symmetrical alignment  
- Even spacing  
- Black linework on light parchment  
- Reiteration of time period and scale  
- No text or labels (to prevent model assumptions)
