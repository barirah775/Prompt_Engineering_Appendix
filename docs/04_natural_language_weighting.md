## Stylistic / Semantic Weighting Tests

These tests evaluated whether qualitative linguistic emphasis produced
more reliable stylistic control than symbolic or Boolean logic.

Instead of symbolic logic operators, natural language weighting terms
were used to influence the architectural character of the generated
façades. The tests examined whether descriptive phrases indicating
dominance, transition, balance, refinement, or residual stylistic
influence produced discernible changes in architectural form.

All outputs shown below are AI-generated experimental outputs used to
compare the model's response to different natural-language weighting
phrases.

## Observations

Across the generated outputs, the stylistic/semantic weighting prompts
produced a greater degree of visual variation than the Boolean logic
formulations. Descriptive stylistic cues generated clearer shifts in
architectural character, particularly in features such as façade
ornamentation, fenestration, window styling, and brickwork.

Qualitative terms such as *predominantly*, *slight*, *subtle*, and other
descriptive weighting phrases were more effective than symbolic or
quantitative weighting in influencing the generated architectural
character. The outputs also showed better adherence to prompt
specifications, including a more consistent orthographic façade view and
appropriate camera framing.

These results suggest that the model responded more reliably to
contextual linguistic emphasis than to logical syntax or numerical
weighting. However, the precise proportional influence of individual
stylistic terms remained difficult to quantify, motivating the
introduction of a computational approach in the subsequent stage.
