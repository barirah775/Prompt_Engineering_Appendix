# Reverse Prompting from Historical Reference Images

This document records reverse prompts derived from historical Sunday School reference images. 
For each entry, the reference image is shown first, followed by a reverse prompt describing its architectural features.

**Reverse Prompt:**  
A detailed 19th-century architectural engraving on aged, yellowed paper depicting a large Victorian institutional building with an eclectic Gothic Revival style. The central tower is prominent, rising with a steep pyramidal roof topped by a finial, and features a clock face and arched openings. Several sections of the building display intricate Gothic ornamentation, including pointed arches, buttress-like vertical elements, pinnacles, and carved detailing around the windows. The left wing has a castellated tower and turret with battlements, while the right wing features steep gabled dormers with circular windows. Small human figures in period clothing walk in front of the building. The illustration uses fine linework, cross-hatching, and subtle shading typical of antique architectural drawings, all rendered on a worn sepia-toned paper background.

The building has a steep triangular gabled roof with ornate finials at each end of the gable and a central decorative feature at the apex. Below, on the ground floor, three tall arched doorways align with the upper arches. Horizontal stone bands run across the façade between storeys, and stone blocks support the window arches. The building includes slightly projecting brick pilasters that divide the bays. The brickwork shows subtle weathering and age, consistent with a late-19th-century industrial-era chapel. The setting includes a low stone wall in the foreground and a paved area with small steps leading to the doors. Lighting is neutral daylight, slightly overcast. Proportions and window placement match the reference building.
